Random HTML Games

Browser games that live in one HTML file each. No installs, no build step, no asset downloads — open the file and it plays. Everything you see is generated at runtime in code.

Neon Run

WebGLthree.js ProceduralDriving

An endless night-city highway racer. The road is an infinite spline built from layered-noise curvature, the city streams in and out around you, and ramps hand you between two levels of elevated expressway. Procedural cars, analytic night lighting, synthesised engine audio — no assets at all.

Voidwake

WebGLthree.js ProceduralExploration

A No Man's Sky–style space explorer. Fly a starship between shader-generated worlds, including a paradise planet, a toxic wasteland and a ringed gas giant with a frozen moon. Dive through an analytic atmosphere, land on a streamed surface and mine crystals for the carbon that fuels your ship. Synthesised soundtrack, no assets at all.
